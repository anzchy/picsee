Design notes

  • Metaphor: an iris/aperture: six blades around one blue pupil. Seeing, not shooting: no camera body, no shutter button.
  • Grid: 1024 canvas, 824 superellipse body (n = 5, 100 px margin). Lens r 322, ring band 64 px, opening r 172, pupil r 120, all on centre.
  • Layers: background (body), fg-1 ring, fg-2 blades, fg-3 pupil. Flat fills, no baked glare. The shadow (y+12, 28 px blur, 30%) is its own group.
  • Applied: continuous corners, Icon Composer layering, four appearances, one blue element, no strokes under 18 px, checked from 1024 down to 16.
  • Small sizes: 16/32 px use an optical variant without blades. Its body, ring, opening and pupil land on the pixel grid; the full six-blade icon begins at 64 px.
  • Risks: it may read as a camera aperture. Clear is faint on plain white.
